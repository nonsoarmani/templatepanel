## Regulatory Requirement Summary
Article 50 enforces strict transparency obligations. Providers of AI systems must ensure that outputs of AI systems are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated.

## Corporate Liability Risks
Failure to deploy valid technical markers or consumer notifications by the regulatory deadline results in administrative fines up to €15,000,000 or 3% of worldwide annual turnover.
